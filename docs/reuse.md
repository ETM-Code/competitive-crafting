## Native interface effects

Retain the existing Web Audio sample player rather than add another audio library. `scripts/sync-sounds.mjs` pins three Minecraft 26.3 sources: `ui.button.click` (7,188 bytes), `entity.item.pickup` (4,487 bytes), and `entity.experience_orb.pickup` (7,815 bytes). The importer verifies source sizes/hashes and caches PCM WAV conversion by source hash, FFmpeg version and conversion settings. `src/lib/audio.ts` maps these to click, placement and collection respectively, reuses decoded buffers and supplies no oscillator/synthesized substitute. Source hashes and rights caveats are in [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md#interface-sound); provenance is not a licence grant.

## Music delivery

Decision reviewed 2026-09-17: **retain authentic music through the official Spotify embed for this delivery**. Do not substitute different compositions or extract provider streams. This review compares documented capabilities and rights, not a new browser benchmark or release acceptance result.

| Candidate                                                 | Decision                                    | Evidence and trade-off                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Official Spotify iframe API                               | Retain                                      | Existing integration preserves provider controls and attribution without hosting music. The [API reference](https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/embeds/references/iframe-api) documents `resume()` and playback state/position events, but notes browser/user restrictions on `play()`. Provider previews, entitlement and autoplay limits remain. |
| App-owned audio player with licensed authentic recordings | Future option, rights/source gate           | Gives the application direct control over playback and mixing with effects, but still needs browser playback permission. No reviewed source establishes a blanket self-hosting licence for the authentic catalogue; obtain suitable permission and an authorized file source for each recording before implementation.                                     |
| Official YouTube embed as a hidden soundtrack             | Reject                                      | [YouTube developer policies](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies) prohibit playback from a background player not displayed on the viewed page/tab/screen, and separating audio from video. This conflicts with the hidden soundtrack needed by the immersive mobile layout.                                                     |
| Original CC0 ambient music                                | Not selected; possible different soundtrack | [Infinite World by Umplix](https://opengameart.org/content/infinite-world) is author-uploaded CC0 music described as “kind of Minecraft-ish”, with downloadable WAVs. It is not authentic Minecraft music. No audio was downloaded, auditioned or imported; substitution would need a separate product choice and listening review.                        |

[Mojang's music policy](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/article/can-i-use-minecraft-music-) allows approved Minecraft-owned tracks in “any non-commercial content” related to Minecraft, and commercial content meeting its Usage Guidelines. The examples are nonexclusive, not a videos-only limitation. The same policy says licensed music requires rightsholder permission that Mojang cannot supply. Confirm applicability and an authorized audio source for this independent game rather than infer permission from a Spotify playlist. The [Usage Guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines) also restrict redistribution of game files. [C418's Volume Alpha Bandcamp page](https://c418.bandcamp.com/album/minecraft-volume-alpha) says “all rights reserved”; purchasing downloads alone does not establish permission to distribute them through an app.

The current interruption mitigation resumes the effects `AudioContext` only when it is not running, and requests `navigator.audioSession.type = 'ambient'` when available. [Audio Session is experimental and has limited browser availability](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Navigator/audioSession); feature detection and exception handling make it optional, not a cross-browser mixing guarantee. Spotify recovery is a bounded timing heuristic for a pause shortly after an application effect while provider controls are hidden. It excludes explicit pauses, mute and reported track completion and limits recovery attempts; it does not prove that effects caused a pause or that physical-device interruptions are fixed. Full playback remains provider/browser-dependent.
